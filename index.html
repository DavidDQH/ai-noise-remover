FAQ

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.

What formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WebM and most common audio or video formats. Output is a standard WAV file.

Is it really free?

Yes. The browser-based noise remover is completely free. A paid plan with studio-grade AI enhancement is coming soon.

How good is the quality?

It excels at removing steady background noise — hiss, fan hum, air conditioning, electrical buzz — while keeping voices clear and natural.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Very long recordings may need a desktop for best performance.
